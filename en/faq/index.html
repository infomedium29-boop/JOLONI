Questions worth asking.

Still have a question? We are happy to discuss your site.

Contact us
Which areas do you serve?

Our services are intended for projects in Zagreb and Zagreb County. Please send your exact location so we can discuss the arrangements.

What services does JOLONI provide?

JOLONI provides concrete transport and concrete pumping. Other construction services are not advertised on this website.

How do I request a quote?

Use the form or contact us by phone, email or WhatsApp. Include your site location, required service, preferred date and estimated volume.

Do you publish fixed prices?

No. Pricing is discussed individually based on the enquiry, requested service and agreed requirements.

What information is useful for a pumping enquiry?

The site location, preferred date, estimated volume, site access and a description of where pumping is needed.

Can I enquire about a small or large project?

You can contact us about projects of different sizes. Available arrangements are confirmed after discussing the requirements and timing.

How far in advance should I enquire?

Please contact us as soon as you have an approximate schedule. Availability is confirmed individually.

Can I request technical details of mixers and pumps?

Yes, contact us about specific equipment capabilities. We do not publish unverified capacities or vehicle reach figures.
